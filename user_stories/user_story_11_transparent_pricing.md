## User Story 11 – Transparent Delivery Pricing
**Title:** Transparent Delivery Pricing  
**Priority:** High  
**Estimate:** 3 Ideal Days  

### Description
As a customer,  
I want to see a clear breakdown of delivery fees,  
So that I understand exactly what I am paying for.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Delivery fee is displayed separately  
- Distance-based calculation is supported  
- No hidden charges  
- Total is clearly itemised  

### Business Value
Differentiates FeedMe from competitors through pricing transparency and customer trust.
