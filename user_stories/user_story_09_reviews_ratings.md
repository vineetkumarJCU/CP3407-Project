## User Story 9 – Customer Reviews & Ratings
**Title:** Customer Reviews & Ratings  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Estimate:** 3 Ideal Days  

### Description
As a customer,  
I want to leave a review and rating after receiving my order,  
So that I can share my experience.

### Acceptance Criteria
- User can rate from 1–5 stars  
- User can leave text feedback  
- Review is linked to a specific restaurant  
- Average rating is auto-calculated  

### Business Value
Improves platform credibility, customer trust, and quality control.
